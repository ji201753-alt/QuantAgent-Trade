# Quant Intelligence Workstation (v1.4)

A converged multi-domain operational intelligence platform for real-time market cognition, forensic investigation, and ecosystem monitoring.

## 🚀 Unified Operational Surface

The workstation transforms complex market microstructure into a unified, multi-domain control surface where intelligence converged from across the core is projected directly into the operator's focus.

### Multi-Domain Workspaces
- **Prediction Markets**: Specialized environment for Polymarket-style monitoring with large layered charts, whale pressure tracking, and catalyst synchronization.
- **Arbitrage Monitor**: Real-time regional spread matrix (AR, BR, CO, MX, EU) and capital flow route analysis.
- **Crypto Intelligence**: Synchronized multi-asset monitor with funding pressure and exchange divergence tracking.
- **Macro Intelligence**: Systemic overview of cross-market contagion and structural propagation.
- **Replay & Investigation**: High-fidelity forensic workspace for building evidence chains and investigation trees.
- **System Diagnostics**: Professional runtime observability for inference latency, GPU utilization, and structural memory indexing.

### Converged Charting & Overlay Orchestration
The charting engine is the central surface of operational cognition.
- **Layered Intelligence**: Individual toggling of Kronos structural analogs, TimesFM forecast envelopes, and liquidity stress zones via the Overlay Manager.
- **Forensic Scrubbing**: Deep temporal navigation that reconstructs the entire analytical stack coherently across all workspaces and panels.

### Operational Intelligence OS
- **Workspace-Aware Copilot**: A grounded reasoning assistant that automatically adapts its analysis based on the active workspace (Predictions vs. Arbitrage).
- **Contextual Command Palette**: Rapid forensic navigation (⌘K) for investigations, analog structural comparison, and overlay management.

## 🧠 Model Orchestration (TimesFM & Kronos)

The workstation supports professional local-first model execution:

### TimesFM Setup
1. **Weights**: Place checkpoints in `checkpoints/timesfm-1.0-200m/`.
2. **Inference Acceleration**: The system automatically detects CUDA-capable GPUs with seamless fallback to CPU.
3. **Diagnostics**: Real-time monitoring of inference latency and hardware telemetry in the Diagnostics Panel.

### Kronos Structural Cognition
- **Structural Search**: Discovery of historical analogs rendered as structural shadow paths directly in the chart timeline.

## 🛠 Platform Architecture

- `/core`: Extensible registry-based orchestration and operational health monitoring.
- `/connectors`: Resilient market adapters for multi-domain ingestion.
- `/analytics`: Real-time microstructure synthesis engines.
- `/forecasting`: TimesFM inference orchestration and trajectory generation.
- `/memory`: Kronos structural fingerprinting and similarity indexing.
- `/investigations`: Forensic case management and publication reporting.
- `/frontend`: Cinematic terminal workstation (v1.4 Multi-Domain Platform).

## ⚙️ Quick Start

1. **Environment**: Run `./install.sh`.
2. **Launch Engine**: `python -m core.engine`.
3. **Launch Terminal**: `cd frontend && npm run dev`.
